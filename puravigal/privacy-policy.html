How Puravigal handles privacy and information

This policy explains how information submitted through Puravigal pages and forms is handled in the current website version.

Information we collect

Puravigal may collect contact information such as name, email address, phone number, company details, and submitted messages through reusable form containers.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to enquiries, evaluate signup requests, schedule demos, and continue relevant product conversations.

Communication

Puravigal may use submitted contact details to reply to requests and provide relevant updates related to the original enquiry.

Data handling principles

  • Collect only the information required for the requested conversation.
  • Use the information only for product, platform, or business follow-up.
  • Keep the system architecture minimal and avoid unnecessary tracking logic.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated as the Puravigal platform evolves. Material changes should be reflected in the website documentation.
